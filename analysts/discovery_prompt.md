# Discovery for the Equity and Index Research Team

This call is an additional discovery sweep for an upcoming full analyst run.
Retain the preceding monitor's discovery, source, timing, contrary-evidence and
broker-data rules. The research context and any earlier watch are fallible inputs.
The common `as_of` is the team’s research cutoff, not its completion time.
Use only information available by that cutoff; preserve unknown publication times.

For this call, return only `checks` and `events` in the supplied schema. Do not
issue a monitoring decision, forecast, trade, dispatch request or referral ID.
Inspect all supplied check groups even after finding a material candidate. This
replaces the normal monitor's instruction to finish once one referral is supported.
Use the actual monitor's checks and discovery windows when supplied; for a first
baseline, use the supplied baseline categories without inventing a prior forecast,
watch state, or a successful coverage checkpoint. For SP500 use market-wide and
material constituent evidence, not a company-only interpretation of the categories.

Actively look for material information the baseline may have missed as well as new
disclosures. Describe older evidence as a baseline correction, never a new market
shock solely because it was found now. Distinguish event date, first publication,
substantive update and retrieval date. Preserve stable event keys and prior IDs.
Do not count syndicated copies as independent confirmation. Compare new evidence
against what is already incorporated, including contrary evidence and mitigation.

For consequential unresolved questions, broaden wording beyond issuer names,
official notice numbers or an expected answer. Search the actual operating issue
in the relevant language and specialist reporting: for example, implementation,
continuing activity, interruptions, delays and usable alternatives. Read decisive
primary sources when accessible. Legal authorization, funding and actual activity
are separate facts. An unsuccessful search does not prove that activity stopped.

Keep this a discovery sweep, leaving financial modeling and deeper investigation
to the research team. Mark partial, failed or not_checked coverage honestly and explain
what could not be checked. Do not fabricate checked coverage to complete the list.
The separate analyst searches remain required after this sweep.
