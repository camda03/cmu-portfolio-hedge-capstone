Reading this evidence

Definitions and provenance

What the dates and returns mean

Histogram dates are GKG observation dates, not verified event dates. T0 is the first trading-session close strictly after that observation date. T20 is the twentieth subsequent trading-session close. Returns are price returns; dividends are excluded.

How the average is formed

Each current story contributes one vote, split across its tied highest-RRF candidates. Historical date paths receive that vote mass as weight. Shared anchors and overlapping periods are dependent observations.

What the analyst contributes

The research role interprets the supplied evidence. It can identify partial analogues or insufficient support. GDELT is an independent advisory input. The receiving decision role weighs this research alongside other sources case by case; no source has automatic precedence.

What the evidence does not establish

Metadata similarity does not verify an article’s full contents. Retrieval scores are not probabilities. The historical average is descriptive. No confidence or prediction interval is implied by these charts.
